SABLE — Portable Demo
=====================

1. Double-click  index.html   → opens in your browser. That's it. No install.
   (Works offline. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari all fine.)

2. Hit  ▶ play  to run the incident. Or  step  through it tick by tick.

3. Click any node to inspect its diagnostic trajectory.

4. Use the scenario dropdown (top) to switch incidents:
     monday_morning   — storage degrades Friday, cascades to Monday login storm
     silent_killer    — slow degradation nobody notices until it's bad
     slow_poison      — wide multi-node cascade
     cascade_whiplash — rapid oscillation
     smd_incident_1   — REAL server telemetry (28 machines)

This is the real SABLE engine's output, captured and replayed so it runs
anywhere. Live recompute on new incidents runs on the workstation build.

See RUN-OF-SHOW.md for the pitch flow.  ROI.html for the numbers.
