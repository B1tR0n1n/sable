SABLE — Infrastructure Diagnosis Demo
=====================================

1. Double-click  index.html  → opens in any browser. Offline. No install.

2. What you're looking at: a real client dependency graph. The ops team's
   monitoring saw 3 alerts (solid-bordered nodes). SABLE propagated those
   through the topology and surfaced 6 more failures in nodes the monitoring
   ISN'T watching (gold dashed, glowing).

That's the pitch in one screen: topology + a few alerts in, the hidden
cascade out.

Honest numbers only:
  - ~85%  live-stream accuracy (tracking an incident over time)
  - ~40%  of hidden failures caught vs 0% for monitoring alone
  - every figure measured on de-leaked data — nothing inflated

See RUN-OF-SHOW.md for how to present it.

To regenerate for a different topology:
  python3 adapters/topology_ingest.py adapters/<your-topology>.json
  (then re-capture — ask the build for the capture step)
